Deliberately Rejected Decision & Omissions
Rejected Alternative & Accepted Trade-off
  • REJECTED: Projected Pipeline Valuation & Rep Revenue Leaderboards

    Considered: Extrapolating dollar deal values from transcript mentions (e.g. $260 partner bounty in M01 or software quotes in M02) to create a cumulative pipeline chart.

5. Trade-offs, AI Usage, Limitations & Future Roadmap

Honest Evaluation of Architectural Choices and Next Iterations

SLIDE 5 OF 5
Architectural Trade-offs & Decisions
Architecture
  • Client-Side Standalone: Zero-install HTML/CSS/JS with in-memory Map store. Eliminates API key leakage, complex setup, and runtime failure; reviewers open index.html immediately in any modern browser.
  • Multi-Route Duplication: Exporting dual-assigned records into multiple folders ensures independent team visibility, accepting slight disk redundancy.
  • Idempotent Revision Logic: Revisions strictly compared; older revisions safely discarded without overwriting manual overrides.
AI Disclosure & Runtime Division
AI Usage Boundary
  • Pre-Generated Analysis: Analysis summaries, quotations, and next actions were pre-generated with AI assistance and stored in data.js.
  • Dynamic Runtime Operations: Deduplication, revision resolution, metric aggregation, route assignment, manual overrides, search, filtering, manifest compilation, and ZIP archiving all execute dynamically in pure JavaScript.
  • Stale Analysis Flagging: Dynamic flag warns managers when underlying transcripts change without refreshed AI analysis.
Known Limitations & What to Build Next
Roadmap & Limitations
  • Known Limitations:

    • Absence of CRM Directory: Cannot cross-reference caller numbers to CRM account histories or verified account values.

    • In-Memory State: Refreshing the browser re-evaluates bundled batches; cross-session persistence would benefit from IndexedDB.

    • Single-Day Scope: All activity falls on 21 August 2026. Trend forecasting requires multi-week time-series data.

  • Unfinished Work / Next Roadmap Items:

    1. Live LLM Reprompting:

    Connect an optional private LLM gateway to dynamically regenerate summaries when late transcripts arrive (e.g. M07).

    2. Webhook Ingestion Pipeline:

    Build a REST intake endpoint validating DATA_CONTRACT schemas for real-time telephony feeds.

    3. Rep Coaching Workflow Tracker:

    Enable managers to leave timestamped audio coaching comments linked directly to transcript turns.
